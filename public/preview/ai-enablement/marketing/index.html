Why marketing first

The fastest wins and the loudest mistakes are in the same department.

Every other team uses AI privately. Marketing publishes it. That is why this function needs more than a licence and a prompt library — it needs a house style the tools are held to, and somebody senior to say what is not allowed out of the door.

The failure we are called in to fix is always the same shape. Output went up, nobody trusts it, so everything gets rewritten by hand anyway and the time saved was imaginary. Fixing that is a question of standards and workflow, not of which model you pay for.

How it runs

Four phases, then it keeps going.

1

Two weeks. Which tools are paid for, which are being used quietly on personal accounts, and where the published work is being rewritten by hand.

2

Write the guardrails

Brand voice, the claims nobody may make, and what has to be seen by a person before it publishes. Agreed once, before anyone is trained on anything.

3

Train on the live campaign

Whatever is going out this month is the training material. No generic exercises, and nothing learned that gets thrown away on Monday.

4

Rebuild the calendar

The month replanned around what the team can now produce, with the guardrails and the review step built into it rather than bolted on.

Straight answers

Questions marketing directors ask.

Will this make everything sound the same?

It will if nobody writes the voice down, which is why that is phase two and not an afterthought. The guardrails exist precisely so the output stops sounding like the default.

Are you going to tell me to sack half the team?

No. The measurable result is hours given back on named tasks. What the team does with those hours is a decision for you, and the good answer is usually the work that never gets done.

Do we have to buy LeadSignal?

No. The programme works on whatever you already pay for. LeadSignal is there because we built it and we know exactly what it does — not because the training depends on it.

Is this different from the general enablement programme?

Same shape, different material. This one is run on campaigns, brand voice and the CRM behind them. If several departments need it, the general programme is the better starting point.
