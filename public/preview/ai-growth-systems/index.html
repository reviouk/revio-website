Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

Why pay you when I could buy the tools myself?

You could, and some people should. What you are buying is the definition of who to target, the data work that makes the targeting real, the connections between the products, and somebody whose job it is to notice when one of them breaks. The licences are the cheap part, and they do nothing on their own — which is why we would rather hold them, keep them current and be judged on whether the system works.

Is this cold outreach, and is it legal here?

Business-to-business outreach to corporate addresses is lawful in the UK on a legitimate interest basis, with a clear opt-out on every message and a record of why each person was contacted. We build that in rather than bolt it on. We do not do consumer cold email, and we will say no if that is what is being asked for.

What about our sending domains?

Separate domains for outbound, warmed properly, with authentication set up correctly so your main domain is never the one that gets burned. This is part of the build, not an afterthought, and it is the single most common reason these systems stop working.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Whichever one you already run. HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Dynamics, GoHighLevel, or something in-house with an API — the data work is the same work in all of them. We are a HubSpot Solutions Partner and know it best, which makes configuration there faster and cheaper, but it has never been a condition. The system is built around your process rather than around your CRM vendor.

How long before it is running?

Two weeks for requirements, then typically four to six for the first working version, depending on what the data work turns up. You see it running on your own records rather than on a demo account, supervised first, before it is allowed to act on its own.

Do you run this on yourselves?

Yes. It is how we fill our own pipeline, and LeadSignal exists because we needed the conversational half of it and could not buy one we trusted.
