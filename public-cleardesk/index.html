What Flo does

Answers the phone

Straight answers

Questions owners ask before they book.

Is Flo a real person?

No. Flo is the name we give the assistant so you and your customers have something to call it. If a caller asks, Flo says it is an automated assistant and offers to take a message or book a slot. Anything it is unsure about goes to you.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot waits for you to type. Flo is connected to your phone, inbox, diary, invoicing and customer list, knows your prices and your tone, and does the jobs on a schedule whether or not you are at a screen. The connecting and the teaching are what we do for the setup fee.

Will Flo send things without me seeing them?

Not until you say so. For the first fortnight every call summary, draft reply, quote and reminder goes past you. After that you choose which jobs Flo may do alone, for example invoice reminders, and which still need your approval, for example quotes. Payments, contracts and anything legal always need you.

What does the monthly plan cover?

Flo's running costs, monitoring, support, and one change a month as your business shifts. Solo starts at From £99, Team at From £245 and Business at From £595, all excluding VAT. Cancel any time. You keep the accounts and the data.

Do I need to change my software?

Almost never. We connect to the phone, email, calendar, invoicing and customer tools you already use. If you have no customer list at all, we set up a simple one as part of the package. We name no software on these pages because the point is that we fit around yours.

Where does my data go?

Flo reads your email, calls and invoices, so this matters. Every account is in your name, data stays in your tools, and the AI services we use process it under business terms, not consumer ones. You get a plain-English one-page note on what it can see and where it goes, and you can disconnect anything at any time.

How quickly is it live?

Solo in about a week, Team in two to three weeks, Business in three to four. The first fortnight after that is supervised, so expect the full benefit about a month after you sign.

What if I am bigger than 40 staff?

Then these packages are probably too small for you. For larger companies, see REVIO at revio.agency.
