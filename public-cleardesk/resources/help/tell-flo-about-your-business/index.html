Tell Flo about your business. Once.

Every agent works from what you tell Flo. Give the details once and change them whenever you like.

Poppy writes your posts from these answers, and the other agents will use them too. Flo can read your website to fill in most of it. Only the account owner can change these answers.

Step by step

Tell Flo in five steps.

  1. 1

    Find the section

    On My Flo, look for Tell Flo about you. It sits near the top until you have finished. After that it is called About you.

  2. 2

    Give your website, or skip it

    Type your address under Your website and choose Read my website. Flo reads the page and fills in what it can. No website? Choose I do not have one.

  3. 3

    Check every answer

    Change anything that is not right. For a business, Flo asks for Business name, What you do, Where you work, When you are open, Your website, What makes you different, How you like to sound, and Questions people often ask, and your answers.

  4. 4

    Choose how you like to sound

    Pick Friendly, Professional or Straight to the point.

  5. 5

    Choose Save

    You will see "Saved. Your agents know this now." If something is still missing, Flo names the answer it needs.

Good to know

Good to know.

  • Business name, What you do, Where you work, When you are open and How you like to sound must be filled in. The rest is optional.
  • Reading your website saves nothing by itself. You check the answers first, then choose Save.
  • Using Over to Flo for your job or for personal use? The questions are different, for example Your job and What you want help with.
  • Later, choose Edit under About you to change an answer. Recent changes shows what was changed.
  • Poppy writes from what you have told it. The more you add, the more the posts sound like you.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

Do I have to give my website?

No. Choose I do not have one and type the answers yourself. A few lines are enough.

Can someone else on my account change this?

Only the account owner can. Other people on the account can see the answers.
