Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

Is this cold outreach, and is it legal here?

Business-to-business outreach to corporate addresses is lawful in the UK on a legitimate interest basis, with a clear opt-out on every message and a record of why each person was contacted. We build that in rather than bolt it on. We do not do consumer cold email.

What about our sending domains?

Separate domains for outbound, warmed properly, authenticated correctly, so your main domain is never the one that gets burned. It is part of the build, and it is the single most common reason these systems stop working.

Do you run this on yourselves?

Yes. It is how we fill our own pipeline, and LeadSignal exists because we needed the conversational half of it and could not buy one we trusted.
