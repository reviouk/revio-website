Help guide

Poppy settings. Your networks, your pace.

Choose where Poppy writes for, how many posts a week you want, and what to talk about.

These settings sit with Poppy under Your agents. Only the account owner can change them. A change applies to the posts Poppy writes next, not to posts already written.

Step by step

Set up Poppy in five steps.

  1. 1

    Find Poppy's posts

    Under Your agents, find Poppy. The box called Poppy's posts holds the settings.

  2. 2

    Choose Posts a week

    Pick a number from 1 to 7. It starts at 3.

  3. 3

    Tick Where you post

    Tick one or more of Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile. Keep at least one ticked. It starts with Facebook. With more than one, Poppy takes turns between them.

  4. 4

    Say what to post about

    Under What to post about, add ideas such as the jobs you do most, seasonal reminders or tips. Under What never to say, add anything Poppy should steer clear of. Each box takes up to 600 characters.

  5. 5

    Choose Save

    You will see "Saved. Poppy will use this for your next posts."

Good to know

Good to know.

  • Want posts now rather than on Monday? Choose Write some posts now. You will see "Poppy is writing..." and new posts appear under Waiting for your yes.
  • A day that already has a post waiting is left alone, so pressing the button twice does not double up.
  • There are limits. Poppy can write for you on demand up to three times a day, and up to 40 posts in a month. My Flo shows how many it has written this month.
  • Poppy plans posts across the coming days, starting tomorrow.
  • If Poppy is paused, Write some posts now is hidden. Switch Poppy back on to get new posts.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

Can I pick just one network?

Yes. Tick one and leave the others. You must keep at least one ticked.

Where do the posts go?

Poppy writes the posts. You copy them and post them yourself, on the networks you chose.
