Help guide

Your week of posts. Say yes, change it or say no.

Every Monday morning Poppy writes a week of posts. Each one waits for you.

Open My FloAll help guides

Each post comes with an idea for a photo. Nothing is posted for you today. You say yes, then copy the words and post them on your own page.

Step by step

From post to page in six steps.

  1. 1

    Look under Waiting for your yes

    Each post is a card. It shows the network, the day, the words and a Photo idea.

  2. 2

    Choose your answer

    Choose Yes, go ahead, Change the words or No.

  3. 3

    To change the words

    Choose Change the words, edit the text in The words for this post, then choose Save and say yes. Choose Cancel to go back.

  4. 4

    To say no

    Choose No. You can say why under Why not? (optional), then choose Send. Poppy will not post it.

  5. 5

    Copy it and post

    After a yes, the post moves to Ready to post. Choose Copy the words, then Open Facebook (or the name of your network) and paste. For LinkedIn the post box opens with the words already in it.

  6. 6

    Mark it as posted

    Back on My Flo, choose I have posted it. You will see "Marked as posted."

Good to know

Good to know.

  • Poppy plans a new week every Monday morning. When you first switch it on, Poppy also writes a first plan once your details are in.
  • Poppy needs your business details first. If Tell Flo about you is not finished, it asks you to finish it.
  • For now Poppy asks before every post. Posting by itself comes later.
  • Poppy suggests a photo to take. It does not make pictures, so the photos are yours.
  • If the copy button cannot copy for you, the words appear selected so you can copy them by hand.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

What if I say no to a post?

It is not used. You can add a short reason if you like, but you do not have to.

Can I ask for posts before Monday?

Yes. The guide on networks and posts a week explains the Write some posts now button.
