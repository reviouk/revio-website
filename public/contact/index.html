Before you write

Things people ask at this point.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one working day, from a person, by email. If the message arrives on a Friday afternoon it is Monday morning.

Do I need to know what I want yet?

No. "Our quoting takes forever and I do not know if AI helps" is a perfectly good message. Finding out is what the audit is for.

Will I be added to a mailing list?

No. The form goes into our delivery system, a reply comes from a person, and that is the extent of it.

Is the first call free?

Yes. Thirty minutes on Teams, no charge and no obligation. If there is nothing worth building, we say so on the call.

What happens to what I send?

It is stored in our own delivery dashboard so that whoever picks it up has the context, and deleted on request. Nothing is shared with a third party. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Can you work outside the UK?

Yes, remotely, for English-speaking businesses in Europe and North America. Prices are in pounds; VAT is reverse-charged where eligible.
