Straight answers

Questions about Tally.

When will it be ready?

We do not have a date to give you yet. Use "Tell me when it is ready" and we will contact you once it is working.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. If you do not have one, Tally shows you how to make one with your own email address. Google asks you to make it yourself.

Does my website need a cookie notice?

Yes. UK rules ask for one when you measure visitors this way. On a website Paige built, Tally adds it. On another website, Tally tells you what to add.

Does Tally change my website?

Only to add the tracking code. Tally reports. It does not change anything else.

How is it different from Finn?

Finn looks at how Google ranks you and fixes the site. Tally shows what visitors do once they arrive.
