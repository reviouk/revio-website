The unglamorous truth

Most of the cost is before anything clever runs.

Agreeing what a lead actually is. Mapping the fields. Deciding what happens to the forty thousand records nobody has touched since 2019. It is the least exciting thing on the quote and the one that decides whether the system works.

Projects that skip it do not fail loudly. They quietly stop being used, and six months later somebody cancels the subscriptions and says AI did not work for them.

How it runs

Scope, build, prove, hand over.

Scope

A fixed statement of what it does, what it does not, and what has to be true first. This is where the price comes from, and it does not move unless you change the scope.

Build

Against your real systems, in stages you can see. Integrations are proven on live traffic early, because that is where these projects actually fail.

Prove

It runs supervised on your own records before it is allowed to act alone. You watch it work and sign it off, or we fix it and you watch again.

Hand over

Documentation, training and the monitoring. Then either you run it, or we do — see the monthly option.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Whichever one you already run — HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Dynamics, GoHighLevel, or something in-house with an API. We know HubSpot best, which makes the data work faster there, but it has never been a condition.

What if the scope changes mid-build?

Then we re-quote that part and you decide, in writing, before it is built. What we will not do is absorb it quietly and hand you a surprise invoice, or build it badly to stay inside the original number.

How long does a build take?

Typically four to six weeks for the first working version, depending on what the data turns up. The scope tells you the date before you commit.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The configuration is documented as it is built, and it is written so another firm could pick it up.
