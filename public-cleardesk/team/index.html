Your week today

You hired people to do the work, and they spend half their day on the admin around it.

At two to ten staff the owner is still the switchboard. Enquiries land in a shared inbox and wait for whoever notices. A quote goes out late because the person who prices it was on site. The invoice reminder is sent twice by two people, or not at all. The Monday meeting runs on gut feel because the numbers live in four places. Everyone is busy and the business still feels slow to customers.

Clear Desk Team puts Flo in front of every person, connected to the shared inbox, the diary, the invoicing, the customer list and your marketing channels. Flo answers and logs enquiries, drafts the replies, chases the money, writes the Monday numbers and drafts the month's posts. Your team approves, corrects and gets on with the work they were hired for.

Straight answers

What owners of small teams ask us.

We already have a CRM. Do we have to move?

No. We connect Flo to the one you have, however untidy. Part of the setup is cleaning it enough that Flo has something reliable to work from. If you have nothing, we set up a simple one. We do not name software on these pages because the point is to fit around yours.

Will customers know they are talking to an assistant?

On the phone Flo introduces itself as an assistant and says so if asked. In email the drafts go out in your team's names after a person has approved them, so they are your team's replies. We never set Flo up to pretend to be a named human.

How does it know which person should handle an enquiry?

From rules we write together in week two: by service, by area, by who is free in the diary, or round-robin. Flo fills in the details and hands over. If it is unsure it asks the owner rather than guessing.

What is in the Monday numbers?

Enquiries received and answered, quotes sent and won, jobs booked, invoices raised, cash collected, overdue invoices with names, and anything that went quiet. One page, pulled from your own tools at 7am. We agree the exact list with you and change it when you want.

Why is the price "from"?

From £1,950 covers a typical team on one site with the usual tools. Three sites, two phone lines or an awkward legacy system cost more to connect, and each person beyond the first two adds £95 at setup. We say so on the first call. The figure we quote is fixed and does not move during the setup.

What does the monthly plan cover?

From £245 a month, ex VAT: Flo's running costs for the whole team, monitoring, fixes, and up to three changes a month, for example a new service, a new person or a new reminder schedule. Two people are included and each extra person is £35 a month. 300 answered call minutes are included; above that, 15p a minute, itemised. Cancel any time.

Where does our data go?

Flo reads your team's email, calls, invoices and customer list, so this matters. Every account is in your company's name, data stays in your own tools, and the AI services behind it process it under business terms. You get a one-page data note and a short access policy for staff, and you can disconnect anything in a minute.

What happens when we grow past ten?

Clear Desk Business adds end-to-end enquiry to quote to invoice handling, 24/7 customer answers, HR and policy answers from your documents, and a monthly review with us. Your Team setup fee is credited against Business if you upgrade within a year.
