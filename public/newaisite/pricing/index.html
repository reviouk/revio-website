What is not on this page

There is no day rate here on purpose.

Selling days encourages both sides to care about the wrong thing. Everything above is priced against a piece of work or a month of capacity, so the incentive is to finish rather than to fill a timesheet.

Tooling is carried on our invoice where the system needs products we have chosen, so there is one number to budget rather than a spreadsheet of subscriptions. On exit, the licences transfer into your name rather than switching off.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

Why publish prices at all?

Because you are trying to work out whether you can afford this, and making that take three calls wastes your time and ours. Every HubSpot-rooted competitor in this market hides them; nearly every automation agency publishes. We would rather be in the second group.

Is there a discount for committing longer?

No. A discount for a longer lock-in is a charge for the right to leave, dressed up. The monthly is reviewed quarterly at the same rate.

What about the tools we will have to buy?

Where the system needs products, we choose, buy and maintain them and carry them on our invoice with a margin. You can see what they are, and they transfer to you if you leave.

Do you work with businesses smaller than this?

Sometimes, and sometimes the honest answer is that the audit alone is enough and you should go and do the two things it tells you. We will say so.
