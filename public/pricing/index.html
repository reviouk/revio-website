What is not on this page

There is no day rate here on purpose.

Selling days encourages both sides to care about the wrong thing. Everything above is priced against a piece of work or a month of capacity, so the incentive is to finish rather than to fill a timesheet. Where scope is genuinely uncertain we say so and price a short discovery instead.

Tooling is carried on our invoice where the system needs products we have chosen, so there is one number to budget rather than a spreadsheet of subscriptions. On exit, the licences transfer into your name rather than switching off. We never take a cut of a subscription, which means you can leave and the machine keeps running.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

Why publish prices when most UK AI consultancies do not?

Because you are trying to work out whether you can afford this, and making that take three calls wastes your time and ours. Opacity favours the seller. Publishing forces us to keep scope disciplined, and it means the number you see is the number you pay.

What does "from" mean in practice?

The minimum-scope version: one process, the standard tool stack, no regulated-data overlay, your existing CRM. Most engagements land at or near the "from" figure. When one will not, we say so before you commit, with the reason in writing.

Are these prices inclusive of VAT?

No. UK VAT is added at the prevailing rate, 20% at the time of writing. Eligible non-UK businesses are reverse-charged.

Is the audit really credited against later work?

Yes. The full audit fee comes off a build or the first month of an Embedded AI Lead commissioned within 90 days. The audit stands on its own whatever you decide.

How does payment work?

Fixed-fee work is 30% at kick-off, 40% at the agreed mid-point and 30% on delivery, on 14-day terms by bank transfer, or by card for work under £5,000. Monthly work is invoiced in advance and reviewed quarterly.

Is there a discount for a longer commitment?

No. A discount for a longer lock-in is a charge for the right to leave, dressed up. The monthly is reviewed quarterly at the same rate.

What about the tools we will have to buy?

Where the system needs products, we choose, buy and maintain them and carry them on our invoice with a margin. You can see what they are, and they transfer to you if you leave. Where you already have the tools, there is nothing to add.

Do you offer revenue-share or success fees?

Occasionally, where there is a clean number to measure against, such as booked calls from a customer-facing agent. It is usually a lower fixed fee with a defined kicker. Ask, and we will tell you whether your case is one of them.

Do you work with businesses smaller than this?

Sometimes, and sometimes the honest answer is that the audit alone is enough and you should go and do the two things it tells you. We will say so.
