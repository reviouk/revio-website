Fit

Who we are useful to, and who we are not.

A good fit

  • UK businesses from about £1m to £50m turnover
  • A CRM or line-of-business system already in place, however untidy
  • A process that happens dozens of times a week and eats skilled time
  • A director who will own the decision and give it an hour a fortnight
  • A preference for one thing working over five things piloted

Not us

  • A FTSE 250 transformation programme with a change workstream
  • Anyone looking for a body-shop day rate rather than a fixed price
  • Businesses that want a tool shortlist and a licence negotiation
  • Projects where nobody internally can grant access to the systems

Straight answers

Questions people ask before getting in touch.

Where should we start?

With the audit if you have several ideas and no agreed order: two weeks, From £1,500, and the fee comes off whatever you build. With a scoping session at £600 if you already know the one process. With an Embedded AI Lead from From £3,500 a month if the work is ongoing and nobody internally owns it.

Do you publish your prices?

Yes, all of them, on this site and on the pricing page. Audit From £1,500, builds from From £4,500, connected applications £12,000, platforms From £25,000, Embedded AI Lead from From £3,500 a month. There is no day rate and no second price list for larger clients.

Which CRMs and systems do you work with?

HubSpot most often, with a decade of HubSpot, CRM and RevOps work behind us, and regularly Microsoft 365, Salesforce, Xero, Shopify, WooCommerce and internal systems with an API. If a system has no API we say so before quoting rather than after.

Who owns the tools and the accounts?

You do. Model subscriptions, API keys and third-party accounts are created in your business name from the start, and documentation comes with every build. Nothing we build depends on a wrapper you have to keep renting from us in order to keep using your own system.

How quickly can something be live?

A single tool or agent is about a week to build, then two to four weeks running supervised, so roughly a month before it acts alone. A connected application is two to three weeks of build, a platform four to six. Dates are fixed at scoping rather than estimated.

What makes you different from a general digital agency?

We build and run an AI product of our own, so the parts that decide whether a system survives its first year, monitoring, cost caps, handback rules, audit trails, are things we have had to get right under real load rather than read about. And we say out loud where AI is the wrong answer.

Do you work outside the UK?

Most clients are UK-based and we are in Borehamwood on the London edge, but the work is delivered remotely with on-site days by agreement. If you sell into the EU we map the EU AI Act obligations alongside UK GDPR as part of the governance work.

What if we already have something half-built?

That is common. The AI app diagnostic at £495 looks at what exists and tells you whether it is worth rescuing or whether rebuilding the part that matters costs less. Either way you get a straight answer and a price before any further work.
