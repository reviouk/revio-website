How Much Does a CRM Cost in 2026? Real Pricing, Hidden Fees and a Sanity Check

6 August 2026

"How much does a CRM cost?" has a sticker answer and a true answer. The sticker is the per-month headline; the truth is what you pay once seats, contact limits, AI add-ons and onboarding are counted. Here's how to run the numbers before you sign.

The five lines on every real CRM bill

  • Seats — per-user platforms charge £15–£90 per person per month. A 10-person team on a "£50" CRM is £500/month. Flat-price, unlimited-user plans remove this line entirely.
  • Contacts or leads stored — most platforms tier on database size. Check the overage rate, not just the included volume. LeadSignal CRM's is £10 per extra 1,000 leads — a number you can find without a sales call.
  • Feature gates — automation, sequences and reporting are commonly pushed to mid tiers. If the plan you can afford can't automate, the real price is the tier above.
  • AI add-ons — increasingly sold per user, per month, on top. Ask whether AI is included in the plan price.
  • Setup and onboarding — enterprise implementations run to five figures. For context, Revio deploys LeadSignal configured to your funnel, typically live within two weeks.

The comparison that matters

Price the platforms at your headcount and database size in year two, not at the demo team's size today. A cheaper headline with per-seat pricing often crosses over within months of hiring — we run that maths against HubSpot in LeadSignal vs HubSpot.
