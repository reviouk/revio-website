How it works

Four steps, all done by you.

    Tell Flo about you

    Tell Flo about yourself once. It can read your website to save you typing. Every agent uses what it learns.

  2. 2

    Pick your agents

    Choose the agents you want, one at a time. Add another whenever you like.

  3. 3

    Decide how much each does alone

    Every agent has a setting called "How much it does alone". You choose it agent by agent.

  4. 4

    Say yes from your phone

    When an agent needs your yes, it waits for you on your phone. One tap and it carries on.

You stay in charge

You decide how much each agent does alone.

Each agent has the same setting, with three choices. Change it whenever you like.

Asks me first

The agent does the work, then waits for your yes before anything goes out.

Acts within my limits

The agent gets on with the jobs you have allowed. Anything outside your limits comes to you.

Acts on its own

The agent handles the job from start to finish and tells you what it did.

Waiting for your yes

Anything that needs you waits in one place.

When an agent has something that needs your yes, it appears under "Waiting for your yes" on your phone. A post from Poppy is one example.

Read it, say yes, or ask for a change. If you would rather an agent did not ask, change its setting. If you have a question, there is a help chat, and a real person can be asked.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before they start.

What is Over to Flo?

Over to Flo is a team of AI agents. Each one takes a job off your hands, such as answering your missed calls or writing your social posts. You pick the agents you want, set them up yourself in a few minutes, and stay in charge from your phone.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You sign up, tell Flo about yourself once, and pick an agent. Flo can read your website so you do not have to type it all out. If you get stuck, there is a help chat, and a real person can be asked.

Which agents work today?

Two work today. Flo answers your missed calls on your existing number and texts you a summary. Poppy writes a week of social posts every Monday in your voice. Flo as an assistant, Bill, Paige, Finn and Lex are coming soon.

What does it cost?

Prices are on the pricing page. There is no setup fee, and nobody needs to call you before you start. You set it up yourself.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes. You can cancel any time. You can also switch an agent to "Asks me first" whenever you like, so it waits for your yes before it does anything.

Will an agent ever act without asking me?

Only if you choose that. Every agent has a setting called "How much it does alone", with three choices: "Asks me first", "Acts within my limits" and "Acts on its own". Anything that needs a yes waits under "Waiting for your yes" on your phone.

Who is behind it?

Over to Flo is made by LeadSignal. You can read how we look after your information and the terms of the service on the privacy page and the terms page.

What if we are a larger company?

Over to Flo is made for individuals and small businesses. For a larger company, REVIO would be glad to help.
