How we connect

Built by us, checked by us.

Straight answers

Questions about connections.

Does Over to Flo work with everything I use?

No, and we will not say it does. The list on this page is what works today and what we are building next. If your tool is not on it, ask us.

Why are some connections not working yet?

Each one is built and checked by us, and some need approval from the other platform. We switch each one on once it is ready.

Will Poppy post to my social pages?

Not yet. Today Poppy writes the posts, you approve them, and you copy and post them. Posting by itself is coming later, one network at a time.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Flo answers on your existing number. You forward the calls you miss.

Which email and calendars will Flo work with?

Google and Microsoft email and calendars first. They are coming next, with the Flo assistant.

Can I ask for a connection?

Yes. Book a demo and tell us what you use. We cannot promise it, but we will listen.
