Why programmes, not workshops

A one-day course is mostly forgotten within a month.

Roughly nine tenths of what people learn in a one-off training day is gone in thirty days. Everyone in the industry knows this, and most still sell the training day.

Adoption happens when somebody keeps showing up: when the awkward question gets answered in week three, when a real task gets rebuilt in front of the person who does it, when the team can see who is actually using it. That is a programme, and it is why we price it monthly.

The programme

Four phases, then it keeps going.

1

Where you actually are

Two weeks. What is licensed, what is used, who the quiet enthusiasts are and what people are already doing in secret.

2

Get the leadership straight

Half a day with the people who set the tone. Adoption dies when the boss does not use it.

3

Train on real work

Your documents, your emails, your reports. Never a generic exercise about writing a haiku.

4

Rebuild the workflows

Short sessions per team, taking one task each and doing it a better way, permanently.
