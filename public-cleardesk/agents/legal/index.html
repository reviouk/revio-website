Questions about Lex.

When will it be ready?

We do not have a date to give you yet. Use "Tell me when it is ready" and we will contact you once it is working.

Do I need to set anything up?

When it is ready, yes, a few minutes of it, and you do it yourself. You sign up and tell Flo about you once.

Is Lex a solicitor?

No. Lex is not a solicitor and does not give legal advice. It drafts and explains. For advice you rely on, speak to a solicitor.

Will it sign or send anything?

Never. Lex never signs or sends anything. You read it, and you decide.

Can I stop it?

Yes. You can cancel any time.

Can it read any contract?

It will read a contract and explain the risky parts. It will not tell you whether to sign.
