How to Choose a CRM: A 14-Day Free-Trial Evaluation Plan That Actually Works

15 August 2026

Most CRM trials die the way gym memberships do: signed up with intent, poked twice, expired quietly. Then the team buys on demo videos and pricing pages — the two least reliable sources available. Here's a 14-day plan that produces a real answer, built around how we onboard teams onto LeadSignal CRM.

Days 1–3: load your real world

Import a genuine slice of your data — 200 real leads, your actual deal stages, your product list. Fake data hides every problem that matters. Pass/fail: could you import and rebuild your pipeline structure without support tickets? (LeadSignal CRM's trial includes every feature of the plan, so what you test is what you buy.)

Days 4–7: run one real process end-to-end

Pick your most common motion — inbound lead to booked call — and run it live: route the lead, log the emails, book the meeting, move the deal. Build one automation and one email sequence. Pass/fail: did a real lead move through a real process with less typing than your current system?

Days 8–11: put the whole team in

A CRM the team won't use is shelfware at any price. Give everyone a login — trivial on unlimited-user plans — and watch where they stumble without coaching them. Pass/fail: by day 11, are reps updating records unprompted?

Days 12–14: judge the outputs

Build the Monday dashboard — pipeline, activity, revenue by source — and price year two at your projected headcount using our CRM cost guide. Pass/fail: does the dashboard answer your real Monday questions, and does the year-two price survive contact with your hiring plan?

Then decide — and commit

If it passed, migrate properly and get help with setup (Revio deploys LeadSignal configured to your funnel, typically inside two weeks — and our CRM scaling guide covers what comes after). If it failed, you've spent 14 days to dodge a year-long mistake. Shortlisting first?
