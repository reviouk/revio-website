The help chat lives inside My Flo. It answers questions about setting up and using Over to Flo, in plain words and short steps.

Step by step

Ask a question in four steps.

  1. 1

    Choose Help

    Once you are signed in, a round Help button appears. Choose it to open the chat.

  2. 2

    Type your question

    Write it in Your question and choose Send. You can also press Enter. Shift and Enter start a new line.

  3. 3

    Read the reply

    You will see "Flo is working on it..." and then the answer. Ask a follow-up in the same chat. It keeps your recent questions in mind.

  4. 4

    Need a person?

    Type your message, then choose Ask a person. It goes to our team, who reply as soon as they can. Choose Close when you have finished.

Good to know

Good to know.

  • The chat only answers questions about setting up and using Over to Flo. It does not give legal, tax or financial advice.
  • If it is not sure, it says so and offers a person.
  • It never asks for your password or card details.
  • There is a daily limit on chat replies and on requests for a person. If you reach it, the chat says so politely.
  • If the chat is resting, you can still write a message for a person.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

Is the help chat a person?

No. It is an automated guide. When you want a person, choose Ask a person.

Can I ask it to change my settings?

It explains where to find things and what to press. You make the changes yourself in My Flo.
