You stay in charge

You decide how much Flo does alone.

Flo will draft and wait by default. Nothing goes out in your name until you have chosen how much it does alone. The setting is called "How much it does alone". Anything that needs a yes appears under "Waiting for your yes" on your phone.

Straight answers

Questions about Flo.

When will it be ready?

We do not have a date to give you yet. Use "Tell me when it is ready" and we will contact you once it is working.

Do I need to set anything up?

When it is ready, yes, a few minutes of it, and you do it yourself. You sign up, tell Flo about you once, and connect your email and calendar.

Which email and calendars will it work with?

Google and Microsoft email and calendars first.

Will it send emails without asking me?

Only if you choose that. The setting "How much it does alone" lets you pick "Asks me first", so drafts wait for your yes.

Can I stop it?

Yes. You can move the setting back to "Asks me first" whenever you like, and you can cancel any time.

Does Flo answer the phone?

That is a separate agent, Flo for phone answering, and it is working today. Take a look at its page.
