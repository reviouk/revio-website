What usually needs fixing

The same five things, in almost every portal.

What most companies get wrong

Every one of these turns up in the first day of looking.

  • The sales funnel has never been mapped in detailNo documented process, no qualification criteria, no automation worth the name.
  • Nobody owns the automation strategySeveral super admins, all building workflows, none of them removing any.
  • Leads are not prioritised by likelihood to buyNo ICP written down, so the sales team works the list top to bottom.
  • Sales and marketing are not alignedTwo teams, two definitions of a lead, one CRM caught between them.
  • No lead scoringEverything arrives looking equally urgent, so nothing is.

What the ones that work get right

None of it is clever. All of it is written down.

  • The sales process is documented and drives the strategyThe CRM matches how the business actually sells.
  • Automation is built by someone who does it for a livingFewer workflows, each one owned and understood.
  • A lead scoring framework existsBest fit and engagement, separated, so the score means something.
  • Sales and marketing meet weekly on the numbersHandover criteria agreed at every lifecycle stage.
  • There is an operational user guideSo the portal survives the person who built it leaving.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

Are you actually freelance, or an agency in disguise?

Revio is a small agency and this is the way of working people ask for by name: you book the consultant, not a team. For bigger programmes there is a team behind it, and we will tell you plainly when a job is one of those rather than quietly billing days for it.

Do you have to be in London?

No. Most of the work is remote, and it is usually the more economical way to buy it. On-site days across London and the South East are there for the things that genuinely go faster in the room — training, workshops, and the first day inside a portal nobody can explain.

What does the first session actually produce?

A written list of what is wrong in your portal, in the order it is worth fixing, with an estimate against each. You keep it whether or not you book anything else.

Can you take over a portal somebody else built?

That is most of this work. Unpicking other people’s workflows, deduping the data, writing down what the automation is supposed to do, and leaving an operational guide so the next person is not in the same position.

Do you do the AI work as well?

Yes, and it is increasingly the reason people call. If what you need is an AI system built on top of the CRM rather than HubSpot consultancy, start with the AI Opportunity Audit instead — it is a different piece of work at a different price.
