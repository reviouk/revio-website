Pause or switch off an agent. Any time.

You can pause an agent for a while, or switch it off. You do both from Your agents.

Open My FloAll help guides

Only the account owner can pause or switch off an agent. Pausing keeps the agent and its payment. Switching off cancels it at the end of what you have paid for.

Step by step

Pause or switch off in four steps.

  1. 1

    Find the agent

    On My Flo, open Your agents. Each agent shows a small label, On or Paused.

  2. 2

    To pause, choose Pause

    The label changes to Paused. Poppy stops writing new posts. To carry on, choose Switch back on.

  3. 3

    To cancel, choose Switch off

    My Flo asks you to confirm. It tells you the date the agent keeps working until, then stops, and that you will not be charged again. Choose Yes, switch off, or No, keep it on.

  4. 4

    Changed your mind?

    After switching off, the agent shows Ends on and the date. Before then, choose Keep Poppy (or the agent's name) and it carries on.

Good to know

Good to know.

  • A paused agent is still paid for. My Flo says so: "Paused. You are still paying for it until you switch it off."
  • A switched off agent keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.
  • The setting called How much it does alone is not a way to stop an agent. It sets whether an agent asks first. Poppy asks before every post for now.
  • If you have phone answering, Pause Bella and I'm away are separate settings for that agent.
  • Choose Change card to update your card.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

Will I be charged after I switch off?

No. My Flo says you will not be charged again. The agent keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for, then stops.

Can I switch an agent on again later?

Yes. It appears under More agents, and you switch it on the same way as before.
