What Is LeadSignal CRM? The AI CRM That Reads What Your Leads Actually Said

27 July 2026

Most CRM software stores what your team typed about a lead. LeadSignal CRM reads what the lead actually said — in email, in DMs and in every logged conversation — and uses it to fill fields, score intent, draft replies and drive automations. It's a full customer relationship management platform with an AI layer built in from day one, not bolted on as a premium add-on.

A complete CRM, not just an inbox tool

Under the hood LeadSignal CRM covers everything a growing business expects from CRM software: leads, companies and deals in one place, tasks and activities logged against every record, email logging with reusable templates, multiple deal pipelines, saved segments and lists, products and line items, quotes with e-signature, and full CRM reports and dashboards.

Workflow automations and multi-step email sequences are included on every plan — so follow-up happens on schedule whether or not anyone remembered to set a reminder.

The AI layer is the difference

Three things separate LeadSignal from a traditional CRM database:

  • AI properties — the AI reads each real conversation and fills CRM fields automatically: budget, timeline, objections, intent. No more relying on reps to key data in. Here's how AI properties end manual data entry.
  • An AI agent on your inbox — connect your email and the agent auto-logs messages, drafts replies in your voice and turns inbound email into tasks. See the AI agent in detail.
  • AI automation actions — automations can branch on what the AI understood, not just on which checkbox changed.

Marketing Studio, funnels and landing pages

From the Growth plan up, LeadSignal includes a Marketing Studio for campaigns and content, hosted funnels and landing pages that capture leads straight into the CRM, and an AI funnel landing-page builder — describe the offer and it drafts the page for you.

Who is it for?

LeadSignal CRM is priced for small and mid-sized teams that find enterprise CRM platforms bloated and per-seat pricing punishing. Starter CRM is £49/month for a solo operator with 2,000 leads. Growth (£197/month) and Pro (£347/month) include unlimited users — the whole team works the CRM for one flat price. A white-label Agency plan is on the way. Full breakdown in our CRM cost guide.
