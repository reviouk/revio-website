They are polite. With "Asks me first" you see each one before it goes, so nothing leaves without your yes.

No. Bill does not give tax, investment or lending advice. It sends invoices and reminders and summarises your numbers.

Yes. Move the setting back to "Asks me first", or cancel any time.

Bill does the asking, politely and on time. It cannot decide when a customer pays.
