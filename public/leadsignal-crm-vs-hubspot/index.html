LeadSignal CRM vs HubSpot: An Honest Comparison for Small and Mid-Sized Teams

10 August 2026

An honest disclosure first: Revio is a HubSpot consultancy and the team that deploys LeadSignal. We implement both platforms weekly, so this comparison is about fit, not tribalism — HubSpot is excellent software that is genuinely the right answer for many teams. The question is whether it's the right answer for yours.

Where HubSpot wins

Ecosystem depth. HubSpot's app marketplace, Operations Hub, multi-hub suite and enterprise governance are unmatched at this end of the market. If you're 50+ seats with complex integration needs, a RevOps function and budget to match, HubSpot (or Salesforce — see our four-platform comparison) is the safer enterprise bet.

Where LeadSignal CRM wins

  • Seat pricing — HubSpot's paid Sales Hub seats add up fast as you hire. LeadSignal Growth is £197/month with unlimited users — the whole company works the CRM for one flat price.
  • Automation on the entry plan — workflow automations and email sequences ship in £49/month Starter. In HubSpot, meaningful automation starts at Professional tiers.
  • AI included, not per-seat — AI properties, the inbox agent and AI automation actions are part of Growth's flat price, not a per-user add-on.
  • Quotes, funnels and marketing in one bill — e-signature quoting, Marketing Studio, funnels and the AI landing-page builder are in the same plan, not spread across Hubs.
  • Time-to-value — less surface area means days to a working system, not a quarter-long implementation.

The honest decision rule

Price both at your year-two headcount (our CRM cost guide shows how). If the HubSpot number makes you flinch and you'd use a fraction of its surface, LeadSignal CRM gives you the 90% you'd actually use at a price that doesn't punish hiring. If you need the ecosystem, buy the ecosystem — and we'll implement it well either way.
