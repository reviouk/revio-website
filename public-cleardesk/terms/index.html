There is no setup fee. You pay for each agent, or for a bundle of agents, that you switch on. Prices are as published on our pricing page when you switch the agent on.

There is no minimum term. You can add an agent, pause one or switch one off at any time in My Flo.

Switching an agent off cancels it at the end of the period you have paid for, whether that is a month, three months or a year. The agent keeps working until that date, and you are not charged for it again. You can change your mind before that date and keep the agent.

We do not refund part of a period that you have already paid for.
