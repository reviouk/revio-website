CRM with Unlimited Users: Why Flat-Price Beats Per-Seat for Growing Teams

1 August 2026

Per-seat pricing sounds fair until you live with it. Every hire needs a licence. Ops and finance "share" a login. The part-timer works from exported spreadsheets. Slowly, the CRM stops being the single source of truth — because half the company isn't allowed in it.

The hidden cost of per-seat CRM pricing

A £50-per-user CRM for a 12-person team is £600/month — £7,200 a year — before add-ons. Worse than the invoice is the behaviour it buys: teams ration seats, so support never logs the complaint that would have saved the renewal, and marketing never sees what sales heard. The data gaps cost more than the licences.

What changes with unlimited users

  • Everyone logs everything — no licence maths, so every call, email and note lands on the record.
  • Handoffs happen inside the CRM — sales to onboarding to support, one timeline.
  • Reporting is complete — activity reports reflect the whole company, not the licensed few.
  • Hiring is free (in CRM terms) — headcount growth doesn't trigger a software renegotiation.
