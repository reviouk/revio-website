Signing in to My Flo. No password needed.

My Flo is your own page. You sign in with a six-digit code sent to your phone or your email.

Open My FloAll help guides

My Flo is where you see your agents, say yes to their work and change your settings. There is no password to remember. Each time you sign in, we send you a code.

Step by step

Sign in in four steps.

  1. 1

    Open My Flo

    Go to app.overtoflo.ai/my/ on your phone or computer. The page is called Sign in to My Flo.

  2. 2

    Type your mobile number or email

    Use the box called Your mobile number or email, then choose Send me a code.

  3. 3

    Enter your code

    The six-digit code arrives by text or email and lasts 10 minutes. Type it into Six-digit code and choose Sign in.

  4. 4

    New here? Create your account

    If you see Create your account, choose it. It takes four short steps: how you will use Over to Flo (for my business, for my job or for my personal life), a few words about what you do, a look at your team, and your name and contact details. Tick to agree to the terms and privacy policy, then choose Send me a code.

Good to know

Good to know.

  • Your first screen starts with Hello and your name, with your business name under it.
  • Below that you will find Waiting for your yes, Ready to post, Your agents, More agents, About you and Recent changes. A section appears when it has something to show.
  • If you have not yet told Flo about your business, Tell Flo about you sits near the top. The guide on telling Flo about your business covers it.
  • Need a new code? Choose Send a new code. It switches on again after a short wait. Use a different number takes you back one step.
  • A round Help button appears once you are signed in. Use Sign out at the bottom of the page when you are finished.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

Do I need a password?

No. Each time you sign in we send a six-digit code to your mobile or email.

The code has not arrived. What now?

Check that you typed the mobile number or email you gave us. Then choose Send a new code. A code is only sent to a number or email that has an account.
