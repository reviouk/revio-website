LeadSignal CRM Features: Pipelines, Automations, Quotes, Reports & AI — the Complete Guide

28 July 2026

Choosing CRM software usually means trawling a comparison table of two hundred rows. This guide walks the LeadSignal CRM feature set the way you'd actually use it — from first lead captured to deal won and reported — with links to deeper guides on each area.

Records: leads, companies and deals

The core CRM records every person, business and open opportunity in one place. Tasks and activities — calls, notes, to-dos with due dates and assignees — hang off every record, so the timeline tells the whole story. Email is logged against the lead with reusable templates for the messages you send daily.

Pipelines that match how you sell

Deals move through visual pipelines, and from Growth up you can run multiple deal pipelines side by side — new business and renewals, for example — plus custom objects on Pro when leads, companies and deals aren't enough.

Automation on every plan

Workflow automations trigger actions the moment a record changes: assign the owner, tag the source, create the task, send the email. Email sequences handle multi-step follow-up on a schedule until the lead replies. Both are included from Starter — automation isn't held hostage on an enterprise tier.

Segments, products and quotes

Saved segments turn filtered views into living lists your team can work every morning. A product catalogue with priced line items feeds straight into quotes with e-signature — build the quote on the deal, send a buyer link, close with a legally-typed signature. No third-party quoting tool, no PDF ping-pong.

Reports and dashboards

CRM reports and dashboards cover pipeline, activity and revenue on every plan — so the Monday meeting runs off live numbers, not exported spreadsheets.

The AI layer

From Growth up, AI properties read each real conversation to fill fields and drive automation steps, and the AI agent works your connected inbox — auto-logging email, drafting replies and turning messages into tasks. The Marketing Studio adds campaigns, content, funnels and an AI landing-page builder.

What it costs
