The problem this solves

Somebody bought Claude seats. Nobody decided what it is for.

A director reads that Claude is good at reasoning through long documents and writes tight code, buys a batch of Team seats, and sends a link round the business. Three months later, adoption is patchy: a few people use it well, most have gone back to whatever they used before, and nobody has decided what it is and is not allowed to see.

Claude implementation is the work of turning a licence into a habit: the right plan, a data-handling policy that survives a client asking about it, training built on the team's own documents, and, where the value is software rather than a chat window, agents built on the Claude API inside the systems people already use.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before they book.

Do we need Claude Enterprise or is Team enough?

Team covers most businesses under a few hundred seats: shared billing, an admin console and a workspace. Enterprise adds single sign-on and audit logs, and matters more once IT or compliance need to see activity centrally.

Does Claude train on our data?

Business plans are configured not to train on your conversations by default, and we set and document that explicitly during rollout rather than leaving it on whatever the account defaulted to.

What is a Project inside Claude?

A fixed space where you attach your own documents and past work as reference. Every conversation inside it can draw on that material, so answers reflect how your business writes rather than a generic reply.

Can Claude read our contracts or long reports?

Yes, that is one of the things it does best. We build workflows that put the right document in front of it, with a defined prompt, rather than relying on someone pasting text in each time.

Do you only work with Claude?

No. We also implement ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, and recommend whichever model fits your systems. We build most of our own agents on Claude, which is why we know it well.

How is this priced?

Rollout and training are billed by the day, from £1,200. An agent or integration built on the API is scoped as an AI Build, from £4,500. Unsure which you need? The Embedded AI Lead covers both under one monthly fee.

What happens to the setup if we stop working with you?

The workspace, the Projects and any agents built on the API are all in your name from day one. Nothing depends on us staying involved.
