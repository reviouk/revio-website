CRM Reports and Dashboards: The 5 Reports Every Sales Team Should Run Weekly

11 August 2026

Most CRM dashboards are furniture — glanced at, never acted on. The fix isn't more charts; it's fewer, tied to decisions. Here are the five reports we build first in every LeadSignal CRM deployment, and the decision each one drives.

1. Pipeline coverage

Open pipeline value ÷ target. Below 3× coverage, this quarter's problem is prospecting, not closing — and you know it in week one, not week eleven.

2. Stage-to-stage conversion

Where deals die. If 60% stall between demo and proposal, the fix is a proposal process, not more leads. Track the trend monthly; a moving conversion rate is your earliest warning of a broken step.

3. Activity by owner

Calls, emails and tasks completed per rep — not for surveillance, but to separate an effort problem from a conversion problem before coaching. (More on using CRM data to manage without micromanaging in our sales accountability guide.)

4. Speed-to-lead

Minutes from lead created to first touch. It's the number most correlated with booking rates and the easiest to fix — pair the report with instant-routing automations and watch it collapse.

5. Revenue by source

The report that sets next month's budget: closed revenue by channel, campaign and keyword. It only works if attribution survives from first touch to won deal — the subject of our revenue attribution guide.

Make the dashboard the meeting

The discipline that makes reports matter: the Monday pipeline meeting runs off the dashboard, live. No exported decks. If a number is wrong, fix the data in the meeting — hygiene improves in weeks once reports are the arena.
