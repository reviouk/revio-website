Your week today

Big enough to have a back office, too small for it to be anyone's whole job.

Between ten and forty staff a business carries real admin weight without the people to carry it. Enquiries are handled well on a good day and lost on a busy one. Quotes depend on one person. Invoices go out late because the job notes are on a phone. The same questions about holiday, expenses and the price list are answered by the owner, by hand, every week. Customers wait until nine the next morning for an answer that was in a document all along.

Clear Desk Business connects Flo to the whole chain: enquiry, qualification, booking, quote, job notes, invoice, reminder, review request. Flo answers customers around the clock from your own material, answers staff from your own policies, writes the numbers every Monday and the full picture every month, and sits down with us once a month to decide what it takes on next. Your people approve at the points you choose. Everything else just happens.

What it does for you

Everything in Team, plus the four things a bigger business needs.

Enquiry to quote to invoice, end to end

A new enquiry is answered, qualified and booked. The site or call notes become a quote in your format. The accepted quote becomes a job, the finished job becomes an invoice, the unpaid invoice becomes a polite reminder, the paid one becomes a review request. One chain, no retyping, your people approving where you choose.

Customer questions answered 24/7

Opening hours, prices, areas, lead times, order status, how-to questions, all answered on your website, by message and by phone, from your own documents and systems, with a clean hand-over to a person when it matters. Overnight enquiries are booked, not lost.

Staff questions answered from your own policies

Holiday, sick pay, expenses, the price list, the process for a refund, who to call when a van breaks down. Your handbook, policies and procedures loaded once, answered instantly, so the owner stops being the help desk.

A monthly review and roadmap

Once a month we look at what Flo did, what went wrong, what it should take on next and what the numbers say. You get a one-page roadmap for the next month and the changes made. It is a back office that improves, not a tool that decays.

Numbers you can run the business on

The Monday page for everyone, plus a monthly view: pipeline, conversion, cash collected, days to pay, who is overdue, which services make money, where enquiries come from. From your own tools, no spreadsheets.

Rules, access and an audit trail

Who may approve what, which customers and documents Flo may see, what it must never do, and a record of every action it took. Written down, agreed with you, and reviewed monthly.

Straight answers

What owners and MDs ask us.

How is this different from the AI Opportunity Audit and a build?

The audit and build route is for larger businesses where the first question is where AI pays at all, and the answer might be a bespoke system. Clear Desk Business is for a 10 to 40 person company where we already know the answer: the enquiry-to-cash chain and the questions everyone asks. It is a fixed package, set up in weeks, at a fraction of the price. If on the first call it is clear you need the audit instead, we will say so.

Which of our systems does Flo connect to?

The ones you have: phone, email, calendars, website, social channels, CRM, quoting, job management, invoicing and accounts. We name no software on these pages because the point is to fit around yours. If a system is genuinely closed we say so on the first call and suggest the workaround, which is usually a daily export.

Who is responsible if Flo gets something wrong?

For the first fortnight a person approves everything. After that you choose what Flo may do alone, and the list is written down. Quotes, invoices and anything to a customer in writing are approved by default. Flo logs every action. When it is unsure it asks rather than guesses. If it does make a mistake, the monthly review exists to find it and fix the rule.

How do staff questions work?

We load your handbook, policies, price lists and procedures once. Staff ask by message, in plain English, and get the answer with the source paragraph. Anything about an individual, a grievance or a decision is passed to the owner or manager, not answered. New policies are approved by you before Flo answers from them.

Why is the price "from"?

From £4,500 covers a typical company on one or two sites with the usual tools. More sites, a warehouse or stock system, several phone lines or a legacy database add work and we price it on the first call. The figure we quote is fixed for the setup.

What is in the monthly plan?

From £595 a month, ex VAT: Flo's running costs for ten people, monitoring, fixes, up to six small changes a month, the Monday page and monthly numbers, and the monthly review with a one-page roadmap. Each person beyond ten is £30 a month. 800 answered call minutes are included; above that, 15p a minute, itemised. Cancel any time. Accounts and data are yours.

What about data protection?

Flo reads customer and staff data, so we treat it seriously. Every account is in your company's name, data stays in your tools, and the AI services behind it process it under business terms. You get a data note, a staff access policy, a record of what Flo may see, and a short data protection impact assessment template filled in for your setup. You can disconnect anything in a minute.

Can it grow with us past forty staff?

Yes, to a point. Past forty staff or when departments start running their own systems, the right step is the AI Opportunity Audit and, usually, an Embedded AI Lead from From £3,500 a month. Your Business setup fee is credited against the first build if you move within a year.
