Straight answers

Questions about pricing.

What is included in the price?

Each price covers one agent, ready to use. There is no setup fee, because you set it up yourself in a few minutes. Every agent asks before it acts, until you choose otherwise.

How do the three ways to pay work?

Monthly is the listed price, paid each month. Every three months is one payment that is 10% less than three monthly payments. Yearly is one payment for ten months, and you use all twelve. Use the switch at the top of the prices to see every amount.

What happens at renewal?

A renewal costs the same as your first payment, whichever way you pay.

What are the phone minutes?

They are the minutes of calls that Flo answers for you each month. Flo, phone answering, includes 100 minutes a month. If you need more, you can add extra minutes in blocks of 100. The price for each block is shown under the bundles.

Can I change my agents later?

Yes. You can add an agent or remove one whenever you like. Agents marked "Coming soon" open when they are working, and the bundles open when all of their agents are working.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel any time.

Over to Flo is made for individuals and small businesses. Larger companies are looked after by REVIO.
