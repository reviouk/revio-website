CRM Workflow Automation: 7 Automations Every Sales Team Should Run

2 August 2026

CRM workflow automation has one job: make the right next step happen the moment a record changes, without anyone remembering to do it. Here are the seven automations we deploy first on almost every LeadSignal CRM rollout.

1. Instant lead routing

New lead created → assign an owner, tag the source, set lead status. Speed-to-lead is the highest-leverage number in sales; routing in seconds instead of hours is the cheapest win you'll ever get.

2. The no-orphan rule

Every open deal must have a next task. Automation: deal stage changes → create a follow-up task with a due date and assignee. Deals stop dying of neglect.

3. Auto-start the follow-up sequence

Lead status becomes "no reply" → enrol in a multi-step email sequence that stops the moment they respond. Persistence without the sticky notes.

4. Stale-deal alarms

No activity on a deal for 14 days → task for the owner, flag for the manager's dashboard. Pipeline reviews shrink from an hour to ten minutes.

5. Hot-lead escalation

When intent shows up — pricing question, demo request, high score — notify the owner and jump the queue. With AI in the loop this gets sharper: LeadSignal's AI properties let automations branch on what the lead actually said, not just which field changed.

6. Won-deal handoffs

Deal marked won → create the onboarding project, task the delivery owner, send the welcome email. The customer's first week stops depending on someone's memory.

7. Hygiene on autopilot

Normalise sources, de-duplicate on email, archive leads dead for 12 months. Small rules, compounding payoff — your reports stay trustworthy.
