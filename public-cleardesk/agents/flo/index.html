Straight answers

Questions about Flo.

Do I need to set anything up?

A few minutes of it, and you do it yourself. You sign up, tell Flo about your business once (it can read your website), and forward your missed calls. There is nothing to install and nobody has to visit.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Flo answers on the number you already have. You forward the calls you miss, and callers ring the same number they always have.

Does it say it is automated?

Yes. Flo tells callers it is automated. It does not pretend to be a person.

What if it does not know the answer?

It answers the usual questions from what you told it. For anything else it takes the details and passes them on in your text summary, so you can ring back.

Does it look after my inbox and diary?

Not yet. Today Flo answers calls, takes the details and texts you a summary. Inbox and diary work is coming soon, in the same Flo and at the same price. We do not have a date to give you yet.

Which email and calendars will it work with?

Google and Microsoft email and calendars first, when that part is ready.

Will it send emails without asking me?

Only if you choose that. The setting "How much it does alone" lets you pick "Asks me first", so drafts wait for your yes.

Can I stop it?

Yes. Tell it you are away, pause it from your phone, or cancel any time. You can also change your opening hours and areas from your phone.
