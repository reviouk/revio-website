Who it is for

It is for people who suspect the answer is small.

Most businesses arriving here expect to be sold a transformation. A fair number of these audits end with “automate two things and leave the rest alone” — which is a real answer, and cheaper to learn now than after a build.

It suits a business with a process that already works and is simply too slow, too manual or too dependent on one person. It suits a business with a CRM nobody trusts. It suits rather less a business that has not yet decided what it sells.

How it runs

Two weeks, and about three hours of your time.

A kick-off call

Forty minutes. What the business does, what is slow, who does it, and what you have already tried. We agree the processes in scope on that call.

We look at the work

Interviews with the people doing it, a look inside the CRM and the tools around it, and the volumes — how many, how often, how long each one takes.

The findings, in writing

Priority order, cost and payback against each, and the honest list of what to leave alone. Sent before the review so you are not reading it at us.

A review session

An hour to argue with it. The version you keep is the one after that conversation.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before booking.

What if the answer is that AI cannot help us?

Then that is the report, and it is the most valuable £1,500 you will spend this year. We would rather write that than build something nobody uses.

Do we have to buy the build from you?

No, and the report is written so you do not have to. It is detailed enough for another firm to quote from, which is deliberate — a report that only we can act on is a sales document, not an audit.

How much of our time does it take?

About three hours in total: the kick-off, the interviews with whoever does the work, and the review session.

Will you need access to our systems?

Read access to the CRM and whatever sits around it, for the week we are looking. Not write access, and not a login we keep afterwards.

How soon can you start?

Usually within a fortnight. The audit itself is two weeks from the kick-off call.
