Straight answers

Questions about Poppy.

Do I need to set anything up?

A few minutes of it, and you do it yourself. You sign up and tell Flo about your business once. Flo can read your website. Poppy then uses it every week.

Will Poppy post for me?

Not yet. Today approved posts are ready to copy and post to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or Google Business Profile. Posting by itself is coming later, one network at a time.

Will it make things up?

No. Poppy never invents prices, offers, reviews or facts. It uses what you have told it.

Can I change a post?

Yes. Each post waits for your yes. You can change the words, or say no and it is not used.

Can I stop it?

Yes. Pause it, or cancel any time.

Who takes the photos?

You do. Each post comes with an idea for a photo, so the pictures are really yours.
