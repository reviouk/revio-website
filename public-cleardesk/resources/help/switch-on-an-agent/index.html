Help guide

Switch on an agent. Pay by card.

You switch on an agent yourself, in a few minutes. You choose how often to pay.

Open My FloAll help guides

There is no setup fee. You pick an agent, pay by card and it appears on your page. Only the account owner can switch an agent on.

Step by step

Switch on in four steps.

  1. 1

    Open More agents

    On My Flo, scroll to More agents. Each agent has a short note about what it does. An agent you cannot switch on yet says Coming soon or Not on sale yet.

  2. 2

    Choose how often to pay

    Under How often do you want to pay? choose Monthly, Every three months or Yearly. The cost sits beside each one. Monthly is chosen to begin with. Poppy is £19 a month. The pricing page shows the rest.

  3. 3

    Choose Pay and switch on

    This takes you to a page where you pay by card. When you have paid, you come back to My Flo.

  4. 4

    Wait a moment

    My Flo says "Thank you. Poppy is being switched on." It checks every few seconds. When it is ready, the agent appears under Your agents.

Good to know

Good to know.

  • Each agent is its own payment. To add a second agent, switch it on the same way.
  • A renewal costs the same as your first payment, whichever way you pay.
  • Under Your agents you will see Next payment on and the date. Choose Change card to update your card or see past payments.
  • If the last payment did not go through, the agent shows a message and a Change card button.
  • If switching on takes longer than usual, My Flo says so. Refresh in a minute, or ask in the help chat.
Quick answers

Quick answers.

Can I pay for a year at once?

Yes. Choose Yearly. You pay once and use all twelve months. Every three months is also an option.

Where are all the prices?

On the pricing page. It shows every agent and every way to pay.
