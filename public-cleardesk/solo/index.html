What Flo does for you

Six jobs you stop doing from the first week.

Answers the phone when you cannot

Picks up on your existing number with "Hi, it's Flo, Dave's assistant", takes the name, the job and the address, answers the usual questions about prices and areas, and books a slot in your diary. You get a text summary within a minute. If Flo could not help a caller, it texts them back so nobody is left hanging.

Sorts the inbox

Reads what came in, flags the two things that need you, drafts replies to the rest in your words, and chases the enquiries that went quiet last week.

Gets you paid

Texts the invoice the moment the job is done, so most customers pay before you leave. Anything still unpaid gets a polite reminder at 7, 14 and 21 days.

Drafts the quote

Turns a voicemail, a call summary or an email into a quote in your format, priced from your own list, ready to check and send from your phone.

Keeps the diary straight

Books, moves and confirms appointments, sends the reminder the day before, and tells you when two things are about to clash.

Remembers every customer

Who rang, what they wanted, what you quoted, when the job was done. One simple list on your phone, so the follow-up and the repeat work do not slip.

Straight answers

What sole traders ask us.

Does Flo sound like a robot?

Flo sounds like a calm, well-briefed assistant, and tells anyone who asks that it is automated. It does not pretend to be you or a person. Customers mostly care that someone picked up, took the details properly and booked them in. We will give you a number to ring so you can hear it before you decide.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Flo answers on your existing number when you do not pick up, or after a set number of rings, whichever you prefer. You can switch it off for an afternoon from your phone.

What if a customer asks something Flo does not know?

Flo takes the details, says you will call back, and sends you a summary straight away. It is set up to say "I will check with him" rather than guess. You decide what it may answer alone, usually prices, areas, opening hours and availability.

Will it annoy my customers about invoices?

The reminders are written in your words, go at 7, 14 and 21 days by default, and stop the moment the invoice is marked paid. You see every one before it goes for the first fortnight, and you can exclude any customer. Most owners find they get paid faster and nobody complains.

I do not have a customer list or a CRM.

Most sole traders do not. A simple one is part of the package: name, number, job, quote, status, last contact. It lives on your phone and Flo keeps it up to date. If you already use something, we connect to that instead.

What does the monthly plan actually cover?

From £99 a month covers Flo's running costs, 250 call minutes, monitoring, support, and one change a month, for example a new price list or a new service. You can pay yearly for £990 instead, two months free. Cancel any time. The accounts and the data are yours.

Where does my data go?

Flo reads your calls, email and invoices, so this matters. Every account is in your name, your data stays in your own tools, and the AI services behind it process it under business terms. You get a one-page note in plain English on what it can see, and you can disconnect anything in a minute.

Can I grow into the bigger packages?

Yes. When you take on staff, Flo Team adds everyone to Flo and shares the customer list. Whatever you have paid for Solo setup is credited against Team if you upgrade within a year.
